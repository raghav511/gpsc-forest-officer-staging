GSSC RFO privacy policy

GSSC RFO is a practice and study app for the Goa Staff Selection Commission's Range Forest Officer exam. Last updated 24 October 2026.

What stays on your device

Your test attempts, tests in progress, custom tests, reading places and settings are saved in your browser on your own device. They are not sent to the app's maker.

Sign in with Google (optional)

If you choose to sign in with Google, GSSC RFO asks Google for two things only:

  • Your email address, to show which account is signed in and to suggest it next time.
  • A private app folder in your Google Drive (Google's drive.appdata permission). GSSC RFO keeps one file there, gsscrfo-progress.json, with your test progress, so you can use it on your other devices. GSSC RFO cannot see any of your other Drive files, and other apps cannot see this folder.

Your device talks to Google directly. Nothing passes through, or is stored on, any server run by the app's maker. Google's one-hour sign-in pass is kept on your device only. You can sign out in Settings, and remove GSSC RFO's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/connections; that also deletes its app folder.

GSSC RFO's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is used only to save and sync your own progress, is never sold, shared, or used for advertising, and is not read by people.

AI assistant (optional)

If you add your own API key for an AI company (Google Gemini, Anthropic, OpenAI or OpenRouter), your questions, and the page text or photo you choose to include, go from your device directly to that company under its own terms. The key is stored only on your device.

No tracking

GSSC RFO has no adverts, analytics or tracking cookies.

Contact

Questions about this policy: write to the support email shown on Google's sign-in page for GSSC RFO.
